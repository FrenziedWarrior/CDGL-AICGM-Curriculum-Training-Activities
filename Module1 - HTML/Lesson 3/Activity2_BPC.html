Planet Earth — Quick Guide

Our planet is full of incredible records, species, and facts. Here is a quick guide using lists!

Steps to Protect Our Planet:

Switch off lights when you leave a room Use a reusable water bottle every day Walk or cycle instead of travelling by car Plant a tree in your neighbourhood Spread awareness — talk to your friends!

Incredible Natural Wonders:

Amazon Rainforest — home to 10% of all species on Earth Great Barrier Reef — the largest coral reef, visible from space Mount Everest — the highest point on Earth at 8,849 metres Sahara Desert — the largest hot desert in the world

Key Terms:

Climate Change Long-term shifts in global temperature caused by human activity Renewable Energy Energy from natural sources like sunlight and wind that never run out Biodiversity The variety of all living species on Earth
